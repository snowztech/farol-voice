# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- An English-only speech model, now recommended if you speak English. It's the fastest, and an accent can't turn your words into another language.

### Fixed

- The app now includes the full license texts of everything it ships with.
- Hear what your agents ask and answer out loud, with no key: yes, no, or an option's name. It's off until you turn it on in Settings, Farol, and needs the next version of Farol.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-01

Settings look like one app.

### Changed

- Settings look the same on every tab: what you can say uses the regular rows, and pickers and buttons match.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-01

Tell Farol what to do from any app.

### Added

- A second key to tell Farol what to do from any app: open Farol, go to a session, message one, or start a task. Needs a Farol with Settings, Agents, Let Farol Voice control sessions.
- Settings has separate tabs for General, Dictation, Farol and Models, and the Farol tab shows if it's connected and what you can say.
- Choose your language, and it recommends the best speech model for it.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-01

No changes to the app itself.

### Changed

- Development builds are now Farol Voice Dev, with their own id and permissions, so they run next to an installed Farol Voice without taking its permissions away.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-01

The first version: dictation that runs on your Mac.

### Added

- Hold a key, talk, and your words appear in any app.
- Parakeet V3 and Whisper models to install, choose and delete. Once installed, they need no network.
- Your own words, so names and terms come out right.
- First-time setup, and a menu bar app with a small waveform in compact or large.
- Automatic updates.
